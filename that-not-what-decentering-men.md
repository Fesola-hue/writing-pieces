I’ve seen a lot of discourse around decentering men, and somewhere along the way, I think the phrase has become noisier than its actual meaning.


Recently, I saw a woman being shamed for posting her husband “too much” because she’s a feminist and apparently feminists are supposed to decenter men. 


But… that’s where I think we’ve started missing the point.


To me, decentering men has never meant not having a boyfriend, a husband, male friends, or even posting the men you love. Feminism isn’t about isolation, and equality doesn’t require pretending men don’t exist.


What decentering men does mean is refusing to make men the center of your identity or your ambitions, It’s about having a full life that still exists in the absence of the men in it.


Honestly? If I could have it my way, I’d say we should decenter everyone. Have an identity outside your relationships. Know who you are before you become someone’s partner, friend, employee, or even someone’s “everything.”


The internet has a way of turning nuanced ideas into rules people are scared to break. Sometimes a confident speaker says something, and before you know it, everyone repeats it without stopping to ask, “Is that actually what this means?”


So maybe the takeaway isn’t just to decenter men.


Maybe it’s to have a spine of your own. Think critically. Know what you believe, and more importantly, know why you believe it.❤️
