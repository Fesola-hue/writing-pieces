I spent my teenage years trying to love myself and each time I did, I failed.

I’ve watched videos, read books, listened to audios and even had a dedicated Spotify playlist that contains all “bad bitch” & “boss bitch” songs.

But they didn’t help. I’d love myself for a few days and then the image would suddenly crash without giving me a warning.

But I felt strongly that if I just “recited” or “listened” to that mantra enough, I’d love myself. So I did that every day, constantly, every hour, strongly. But I never actually did. I just pretended I did and ran with it. But hey, I’d end up exposing myself in a few days because I just couldn’t hide that hateful bitch away.

So, after years of trying, I finally decided to stop. Instead, I wanted to know why it all seemed to work out for everyone except me. Why does everyone love themselves except me? Was I cursed? Or was I just born hateful?

The answer? None!

In fact, it did work for me because I wasn’t trying to love myself. I wanted to pretend I did. I wanted to manifest my way into loving myself. I believed it was something I was born with and I just had to invoke it like everyone else.

So did I ever discover the problem? Or did I just keep pretending?

Yes, I discovered the reason I hadn’t loved myself, or maybe the reason it was so hard to.

My entire life was a book filled with the words of everyone around me. They picked a thing about me and wrote down tales on how I should live, feel, eat, drink and go on about my life. The pen, the paper, neither the words were mine.

So I was trying to earnestly love a version of myself I couldn’t understand nor explain because I didn’t write the book. But the book was deciding how I should live, what I should like and, quite frankly, who I was without ever needing my input.


And the unfortunate part? It never grew with me.

So, what was the solution? What did you do?

I wanted to write my own book. So, i got my own blank paper

But doing that was hard. Starting on a blank page, I mean. I felt empty, sometimes losing the will to live, but I didn’t want to go back to the book that wasn’t mine.

So, I decided to date myself.

You know how you date someone you barely know and just get to know them through different experiences and ways, right? Yes, that’s what I did.

It was fascinating, fun and I had a reason to live. I was always so excited to write in my journal like, “hey, I just found out Hayyi loves milk, haha.” It was really interesting, but sometimes it wasn’t.

I realized loving myself was never meant to be linear or at 100%, and I started making peace with the days I didn’t feel like loving myself.

Because it was more about loving yourself even when it’s hard, slow, bad, sad, not just on the happy days.

It was being willing to be you even when the storm hits. Choosing yourself in the face of adversity. Loving you on the happiest days and, most importantly, writing different chapters for different versions of your life, in your own words, with your own pen.

Because life is however you define it, and your life is shaped by the books and lenses you use to live it.

If you’re going to live through a book anyway, you might as well have a hand in writing it.

And yes, you can’t manifest your way into it. The same way you never manifested your way into loving a partner. You just do, you just learn, you just practice.
